The Method

Missional Bible Reading

Genesis and Exodus are the fruit of this method — not the whole of it. The method itself can be applied to any passage of Scripture.

Built on two interpretive foundations — historical grounding and transcendent meaning — this method guides you to read each week's passage through three perspectives, together within the work of the Triune God.

01 · God-Centered

What does this reveal about God?

Reads the passage first for God's character, will, and mission — and asks how our lives should respond to that God.

02 · Christ-Centered

How is this fulfilled in Jesus?

Reads the passage within God's whole redemptive story, tracing how it is fulfilled in the mission of God accomplished in Christ.

03 · Spirit-Centered

How does the Spirit lead us to respond?

Connects the rightly-understood text to today's life and community under the Spirit's guidance, leading the church into God's mission.
