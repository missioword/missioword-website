Why MissioWord

Not a resource library. A formation ministry.

MissioWord is not limited to one book of the Bible. It is a way of reading the whole Bible through the mission of God — guided by a God-centered, Christ-centered, and Spirit-centered perspective.

The Method

Missional Bible Reading

Genesis and Exodus are the fruit of this method — not the whole of it. The method itself can be applied to any passage of Scripture.

01 · Biblical Story

What story is the Bible telling?

The single, unfolding story of God's mission — from creation to new creation.

02 · Trinitarian Lens

How do we read a passage?

Through a God-centered, Christ-centered, and Spirit-centered reading of the text.

03 · 6R Pathway

Where does the reading lead?

From formation, to practice, to raising up leaders, to reproduction among the nations.
