Why MissioWord

Not a resource library. A formation ministry.

MissioWord is not limited to one book of the Bible. It is a way of reading the whole Bible through the mission of God.

The Need

We read the Bible, but often fail to connect it to God's mission story — or to our own place within it.

Our Conviction

The whole Bible, from creation to new creation, is the story of God's mission.

Our Difference

Not resources to consume, but people and churches formed to reproduce — from personal devotion to church planting and unreached peoples.

See the Vision in 2 Minutes — what missional Bible reading is, and why it matters for God's mission.

The Method

Missional Bible Reading

Genesis and Exodus are the fruit of this method — not the whole of it. The method itself can be applied to any passage of Scripture.

Two Foundations

Historical Grounding

Careful attention to each passage's historical, literary, and theological meaning.

Transcendent Meaning

Reading each passage within the whole of Scripture and the mission of the Triune God.

Three Perspectives

Read every passage through three perspectives, together within the work of the Triune God.

01 · God-Centered

What does this reveal about God?

Reads the passage first for God's character, will, and mission — and asks how our lives should respond to that God.

02 · Christ-Centered

How is this fulfilled in Jesus?

Reads the passage within God's whole redemptive story, tracing how it is fulfilled in the mission of God accomplished in Christ.

03 · Spirit-Centered

How does the Spirit lead us to respond?

Connects the rightly-understood text to today's life and community under the Spirit's guidance, leading the church into God's mission.

The 6R Formation Pathway

The whole journey, in one shape: Read → Reframe → Raise Up → Reproduce → Reach Out → Renew.
